# Caleb's Wedding Checklist (Time-Based)

Same milestone structure as the main checklist, but trimmed down to just the tasks
that are explicitly his (from the Binder's "Caleb's To Do List") plus other tasks
in the documents that clearly belong to him (his suit, groomsmen, bachelor party,
his role on the wedding day). A few borderline items are called out at the end for
you two to decide who owns them — I didn't want to assign them without checking.

## Priority / Early (No Fixed Month)
- Get Addresses and Phone Numbers For All Guests
- Send Save the Dates
- Talk to Parents About Rehearsal/Welcome Party

## 8 Months Out (September)
- Order Groomsmen Suits
- Get His Suit (buying, not renting)

## 7 Months Out (October)
- Start Dance Lessons With Ricky (first dance choreography)
- Write His Vows

## 2 Months Out (March)
- Bachelor Party

## 1 Month Out (April)
- Venue Walkthrough With Vendors
- Help Finalize Day Of Timeline With Vendors

## 1 Week Out (April 24)
- Rehearsal
- Rehearsal Dinner/Welcome Party
- Pack For the Hotel
- Pack For the Honeymoon

## Wedding Day (May 1)
- Phone On Silent
- Give the Rings to the Best Man
- Give the License and Tip Envelopes to the Planner

## After the Wedding
- Honeymoon
- Name Change Paperwork *(if applicable on his side, e.g. any joint filing steps)*

---

## Needs a Decision: Whose Task Is This?
These showed up in the source documents without a clear owner — worth a quick
conversation before they go in either checklist (or both, as a shared task):
- Get the Rings *(traditionally groom-driven, but check who's actually handling it)*
- Ask Flower Girls
- Marriage License / Legal Documentation
- Ask the Ushers
- Officiant coordination *(his dad is officiating, so he may be the natural point of contact)*
